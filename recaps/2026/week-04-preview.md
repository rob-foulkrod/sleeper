# Week 4 Preview: The Perfect Records Meet the Winless Chase

_The League, 2026. Filed October 2. Current rosters, provisional lineups, and availability come from the dated Week 3-final/Week 4-ahead snapshot. Picks are editorial entertainment._

Rob and Dave enter 3-0. Michael and Jake enter 0-3. Week 4 pairs each unbeaten team with a winless one, which is either a clean sorting mechanism or exactly how the schedule tempts a columnist into overconfidence. The middle gives us Devin against Jake and a genuine bubble game between Brian and Travon.

I went **2-2** on last week's winners. Devin and Dave handled their assignments. I was wrong on Brian over Rob and Michael over Eric; Rob made the first miss look especially ambitious. The full accounting is in the [Week 3 recap](/sleeper/2026/week-03/). Four more picks are now on the record.

## Matchup Capsules

### Rob vs Michael: First Against the Injury Report

**Unstoppable Farce (3-0) vs Broken leg broken team (0-3)**

The league leader meets a winless team without Justin Jefferson, DeVonta Smith, or Breece Hall. The [Vikings' Friday report](https://www.vikings.com/news/dolphins-injury-report-week-4-2026-nfl-season) rules Jefferson out with an ankle injury, and the [NFL Week 4 status report](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-week-4-injury-report-player-statuses-for-all-16-games) lists Hall out with a quadriceps injury. Sleeper lists Smith out with a hamstring injury.

Rob's backfield of Derrick Henry and Kenneth Walker helps create the 30.6-point projected edge, and Jalen Hurts enters the best projected legal lineup. Rob has his own longer-term problem: De'Von Achane, who started for him in Week 3, tore his ACL and [will miss the rest of the season](https://www.nfl.com/news/dolphins-rb-devon-achane-miss-2026-season-acl-tear). Sleeper also lists Jalen Coker and Zay Flowers questionable.

Michael's upset route runs through Jared Goff and Deshaun Watson combining for ceiling games while Rob's questionable receivers sit. Keenan Allen, one of Michael's possible fill-ins, is also questionable.

**My pick: Rob.** The injury gap is too large to treat Michael's theoretical best lineup as the likely outcome.

### Devin vs Jake: The Week's Largest Projection Gap

**Amon Another level (2-1) vs Crazy J’s Stable of Stars (0-3)**

Devin climbed to No. 2 in the power rankings and carries the league's best three-week scoring average at 203.77. Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Bijan Robinson give him enough cushion to absorb Jadarian Price's chest injury, with Kyle Monangai and Chris Rodriguez available in the backfield. Sleeper lists Mike Evans questionable with a ribs injury.

Jake needs six provisional bench players in the best projected legal lineup. Ladd McConkey and Kenyon Sadiq are both questionable, while the [Buccaneers' Friday report](https://www.buccaneers.com/news/buccaneers-packers-injury-report-oct-2-week-4-2026) rules Baker Mayfield out with a right thumb dislocation. Jake's path starts with a huge Bryce Young game and needs Devin's replacements to miss.

**My pick: Devin.** The model's 48.7-point gap is the largest of the week, and Devin just led the league with 217.34.

### Dave vs Eric: Perfect Record, Real Counterpunch

**Early and out of hope (3-0) vs Boutte on Sunday (1-2)**

Dave projects to win by 19.7. Josh Allen gives him the highest single-player projection on the slate, and the optimizer moves Kirk and Dontayvion Wicks into the best projected legal lineup.

Eric has the week's clearest upset lever in Jahmyr Gibbs, fresh off a league-best 34.40. His best projected lineup also draws on the Raiders defense, Tre Tucker, and Wan'Dale Robinson. A two-score Gibbs day and a defensive return could erase the gap if Allen lands closer to an ordinary week.

**My pick: Dave.** Eric has a real countercase, but Dave has reached 3-0 without needing Allen to hit his ceiling every week.

### Brian vs Travon: The No. 4 Seed Is Already in Play

**Tri-Lams (2-1) vs Von (1-2)**

This is the closest game on the board: one place separates them in the power rankings, and Travon projects to win by about 10. Brian currently holds the No. 4 seed after falling two places in the power rankings. His best projected legal lineup moves Cam Ward in from the provisional bench.

Travon has the stronger quarterback projection with Brock Purdy and Patrick Mahomes, plus Jonathan Taylor in the backfield. The volatility sits on his side too: Sleeper lists Terry McLaurin and Tyjae Spears questionable. If both sit and Ward beats his projection, Brian can keep the seed outright. If Travon wins, both teams leave Week 4 at 2-2.

**My pick: Travon, narrowly.** Brian's Cam Ward route makes this uncomfortable.

## Predictions

- **Rob and Dave both win and finish Week 4 as the league's only 4-0 teams.** Michael and Jake both lose and fall to 0-4, tightening the early race to avoid last place and its lost keeper slot.
- **Unstoppable Farce scores at least 200 and beats Broken leg broken team by 25 or more.** That would come in Rob's first week without Achane and with both Coker and Flowers questionable.
- **Devin wins by at least 35, the largest margin of the week.** That would follow his two-place power climb with another 200-point week built around Smith-Njigba, even with Price out.
- **Dave beats Eric by double digits.** Allen and Kirk outscore Eric's Gibbs-plus-Raiders formula by at least 10.
- **Travon beats Brian by fewer than 15 points.** Von pulls level with Tri-Lams at 2-2 and hands Brian a second straight loss.
