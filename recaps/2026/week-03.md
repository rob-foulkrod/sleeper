# Week 3 Recap: Benches Burn, Unbeaten Teams Separate

_The League, 2026. Filed October 2 after all Week 3 NFL games finished. Fantasy totals are from Sleeper and remain subject to stat corrections. Injury reporting runs through 7:33 p.m. Eastern on October 2._

Eric's 186.16-159.02 win over Michael was the narrowest game of Week 3, and the margin was still 27.14 points. It was also the one game where lineup decisions had enough leverage to change the winner. Michael's bench held enough points to flip the result, including Deshaun Watson's 20.26, so Broken leg broken team fell to 0-3 with the winning lineup sitting unused. Jahmyr Gibbs delivered the week's top individual score at 34.40, and Eric reached 1-2 in the first week since "Sanders" left the nameplate. Boutte on Sunday climbs to #6. Michael drops to #7.

Three weeks in, The League has split into a clear top and a clear floor, and the floor is being set by lineup decisions as much as by talent. Jake lost 189.46-150.70 to Dave with a bench that held a 52.00-point answer to a 38.76-point deficit. That makes two winless teams whose rosters could have won and whose Sunday lineups did not. Above them, Rob and Dave are both 3-0, and Devin posted the week's best total, 217.34, in a 66.52-point rout of Travon. Their 368.16 combined points made it the highest-scoring game of the week. The good teams are scoring. The 0-3 teams are leaving points behind.

## Around the League

Rob's 202.82-142.22 win over Brian keeps Unstoppable Farce alone at #1 and at 3-0. It also ended Tri-Lams' unbeaten start. Brian posted the week's low score and fell two spots to #4, with Dalton Kincaid's 1.8 on a 14.3-point projection among the worst disappointments of the week. Dave matched Rob at 3-0 and moved up to #2 even though Josh Allen scored 16.5 against a 38.2-point projection. Early and out of hope keeps winning while its quarterback underdelivers, which says something about the depth behind him and probably something about the team name.

Devin made the biggest move in the power rankings, up two spots to #2. Bijan Robinson scored 33.30 on a 15.7-point projection, Jaxon Smith-Njigba added 25.36, and Amon Another level now leads The League with 611.30 points for, ahead of Rob's 603.18. Travon got 31.28 from Brock Purdy and still lost by 66.52. Jake's Sam Darnold produced the strangest line of the week: 27.66 points on a 0.3-point projection, in a loss.

Marcus Mariota was the week's most productive waiver addition at 20.42 points. Michael claimed him and dropped Drake Maye. Rob made the most consequential move. He dropped De'Von Achane, who started for him in Week 3, and claimed MarShawn Lloyd, who scored 1.8.

## Game by Game

### Boutte on Sunday (186.16) vs Broken leg broken team (159.02)

**Michael's Bench Held the Win: Deshaun Watson's 20.26 Sat Out in a 27.14-Point Loss**

Eric's Boutte on Sunday beat Michael's Broken leg broken team 186.16 to 159.02, and the result turned on a lineup call. Michael started Drake Maye at quarterback and got 3.76. Deshaun Watson sat on his bench and scored 20.26. The data shows Michael's bench optimization was worth 45.60 points, well past the 27.14-point margin, so a perfect lineup would have flipped this game. The Maye-for-Watson swap alone accounts for 16.50 of that. Cameron Dicker's 10.00 at kicker and Keenan Allen's 12.30 at wide receiver were also waiting on that bench, ready to replace starters who delivered 5.00 and 3.20.

Eric earned the win with Jahmyr Gibbs, who produced 34.40 on 99 rushing yards, two rushing touchdowns and a receiving touchdown, the top score of the matchup. Joe Burrow added 22.58, Drake London 19.40 on 194 receiving yards, Harold Fannin 17.10 on two touchdowns, and the Jacksonville defense 17.00. Plenty of Eric's lineup went quiet, including Tetairoa McMillan at 1.70 and Blake Corum at 1.50, and the top-end production covered for it. Michael got 19.40 from James Cook and 15.00 from Bhayshul Tuten, and Justin Jefferson's 3.20 and Stefon Diggs's 3.30 left the middle of the roster thin. Michael's score ranked fifth in the league and Eric's ranked fourth.

Eric moves to 1-2 and Rank #6 with a W1 streak. Michael falls to 0-3 and Rank #7, with a three-game losing streak, after a week in which his own bench outscored the margin that beat him.

## Storylines

- Michael faces the 3-0 leader without Jefferson or Smith, and a fourth straight loss would put Broken leg broken team firmly in the race to avoid the last-place keeper penalty.
- Jake also gets a top-three team in Devin, and he does so without Mayfield, which means the lineup decisions that cost him Week 3 have to go right just to keep Crazy J's off the bottom alone at 0-4.
- Rob's first game without Achane will show whether a 3-0 team can absorb a season-ending loss, or whether Rob just felt the first real crack in his season.
- Dave puts his 3-0 start on the line against an Eric team coming off Gibbs' 34.40, while Brian and Travon play for the #4 seed. That bubble game is the first chance for anyone to take a playoff spot from the current top four, and the open question is whether Tri-Lams' 60-point loss was a blip or the start of a fall.
