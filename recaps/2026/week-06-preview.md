# Week 6 Preview: Depth Gets the First Word

_The League, 2026. Filed October 13. Current ownership, provisional starters, NFL opponents, and availability were checked through 5:01 p.m. Eastern. Picks are editorial entertainment._

Rob enters Week 6 at 5-0 after scoring 287.10. Devin and Brian are both 4-1 and now have to go through each other. Michael finally owns a win and immediately draws the league's only remaining winless team. The schedule has put pressure at every seam: first place can pull farther away, one of the closest pursuers must take a second loss, and the bottom two teams meet with the keeper penalty already visible in the distance.

## Matchup Capsules

### Rob vs Dave: The Unbeaten Team Has Replacements Ready

**Unstoppable Farce (5-0) vs Early and out of hope (3-2)**

Rob loses Chase and Cincinnati's defense to byes, but the provisional roster still offers Drake Maye against the Jets, Trevor Lawrence against Houston, Derrick Henry and Zay Flowers at Cleveland, Jaylen Warren at Tampa Bay, and Davante Adams against Arizona. Week 5 showed what happens when several of those positions hit at once.

Dave counters with Josh Allen at Las Vegas, Matthew Stafford against Arizona, Travis Kelce against the Chargers, Christian Watson against Dallas, and D'Andre Swift at Atlanta. Rashee Rice is questionable with a hamstring designation, so Dave's upset route leans heavily on another large quarterback total and cleaner production from the lower lineup slots.

**My pick: Rob.** The byes lower the ceiling without erasing the depth advantage. I expect Unstoppable Farce to reach 6-0 and clear 200 points again.

### Devin vs Brian: One of the Chasers Has to Blink

**Amon Another level (4-1) vs Tri-Lams (4-1)**

Devin can build around Dak Prescott at Green Bay, Aaron Rodgers at Tampa Bay, Bijan Robinson against Chicago, Kyren Williams against Arizona, and Mike Evans against Washington. Jaxon Smith-Njigba is questionable with an ankle designation before Seattle visits Denver, while Will Reichard and Tee Higgins are on bye.

Brian has Jordan Love against Dallas, Christian McCaffrey against Washington, Bucky Irving against Pittsburgh, Nico Collins at Jacksonville, and CeeDee Lamb at Green Bay. The injury burden is heavier. Deebo Samuel and TreVeyon Henderson are listed out, and Michael Penix and Lamb are questionable. Brian still has star power; he also has more lineup holes asking for immediate answers.

**My pick: Devin.** Amon Another level has the cleaner route to 18 useful starters. I expect Devin to win by double digits and take sole possession of second place.

### Michael vs Jake: A First Win Meets the Last Zero

**Broken leg broken team (1-4) vs Crazy J's Bed & Breakfast (0-5)**

Michael follows his first win with James Cook at Las Vegas, Tyler Warren against Tennessee, Parker Washington against Houston, DK Metcalf at Tampa Bay, and Tyler Shough at the Giants. DeVonta Smith is listed out and Minnesota's defense has a bye, but the roster still carries the stronger recent scoring base.

Jake has Puka Nacua against Arizona, Malik Nabers against New Orleans, Ashton Jeanty against Buffalo, Hunter Henry against the Jets, and quarterbacks Sam Darnold at Denver and Bryce Young at Philadelphia. Saquon Barkley is listed out, while Ladd McConkey is questionable with a foot designation.

**My pick: Michael.** Cook, Warren, and Washington give Broken leg broken team enough current form to make the first win a two-game streak. Jake falls to 0-6 unless the quarterback slots dramatically beat expectation.

### Eric vs Travon: The Bye Week Picks a Side

**Boutte on Sunday (2-3) vs Von (1-4)**

Eric's provisional starters still show Joe Burrow, Jahmyr Gibbs, Jameson Williams, and Chase Brown, but all four are on bye. C.J. Stroud at Jacksonville, Drake London against Chicago, Tucker Kraft against Dallas, and Baltimore's defense at Cleveland become the foundation of a lineup that needs replacements almost everywhere else.

Travon answers with Patrick Mahomes against the Chargers, Brock Purdy against Washington, Jonathan Taylor against Tennessee, Trey McBride at the Rams, and Chris Olave against the Giants. Quinshon Judkins is questionable with a lower-leg designation, but Travon's available core is substantially more intact.

**My pick: Travon.** The schedule has taken too large a bite out of Eric's best lineup. Von ends its three-game losing streak, though the projection keeps this as the closest call of the four.
