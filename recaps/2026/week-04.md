# Week 4 Recap: Javonte Checks In, Dave Checks Out of Perfect

_The League, 2026. Filed October 11. Week 4 results cover October 1-5; evidence was checked through 3:15 p.m. Eastern on October 11. Week 5 is already underway, so this edition closes Week 4 without new predictions._

Rob bought a running back on Wednesday and got three touchdowns on Sunday. Javonte Williams arrived from Jake in a player-and-pick deal, scored **26.30**, and helped Unstoppable Farce beat Michael **209.32-167.00**. Rob is the league's only remaining unbeaten owner at **4-0**.

Dave lost his share of perfection in considerably less comfortable fashion. Eric's **198.40-145.90** win brought Boutte on Sunday to 2-2 and gave Early and out of hope its first defeat. Tetairoa McMillan supplied 31.20, the highest starter score in the league. The team name finally has a grievance it can document.

Devin took the weekly scoring crown at **209.48**, just **0.16** ahead of Rob, and beat Jake by **74.80**. Brian recovered from his Week 3 loss with **205.70** against Travon's **153.92**. Three owners now sit at 3-1 behind Rob; Michael and Jake remain winless.

## Game by Game

### Rob 209.32, Michael 167.00: The Trade Paid Its First Installment

**Unstoppable Farce moves to 4-0; Broken leg broken team falls to 0-4.**

Kenneth Walker's **29.90** and Javonte Williams' **26.30** gave Rob 56.20 from two running-back slots. Williams' three short-yardage touchdowns in [Dallas' win over Houston](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-season-week-4-what-we-learned-from-sunday-s-games) were an immediate return on the September 30 trade. Add Rhamondre Stevenson's **15.40**, Derrick Henry's **13.90**, and Jaylen Warren's **12.60**, and Rob's five starting running backs combined for **98.10**.

That carried a receiver group with some very expensive quiet afternoons. Ja'Marr Chase and Garrett Wilson each scored **2.70**, Davante Adams had **3.20**, and George Pickens supplied **7.50**. Zay Flowers broke up that pattern with **17.80**. Brock Bowers added **14.60** at tight end. Rob won without starting Jalen Hurts: Tyler Shough and Trevor Lawrence delivered **15.94** and **13.08**.

Michael got **24.90** from Chuba Hubbard, **20.48** from Jared Goff, and **14.30** from James Cook. His kickers, Jake Bates and Cam Little, contributed **30.00** between them. The receiving lineup had two holes before it could build much momentum: Justin Jefferson and DeVonta Smith were both ruled out for Week 4 and both remained in Michael's starting lineup, producing zero. DK Metcalf's **11.50** and Devaughn Vele's **11.40** helped, but Stefon Diggs and Parker Washington combined for only **4.50**.

Michael did start Deshaun Watson this time, getting **12.92** after Watson's Week 3 points had sat on the bench. Rob had his own unused points: Drake Maye's **26.16** and Brian Robinson's **24.70** were on his bench. Even with that cushion untouched, Rob posted his third 200-point outing in four weeks.

Michael improved his score for the second straight week, from **157.72** to **159.02** to **167.00**, though he remains below his **169.18** opener. He has four losses to show for those four efforts. The latest progress is real; the schedule has declined to reward it.

### Devin 209.48, Jake 134.68: Two 26.70s and a Vacant Room

**Amon Another level improves to 3-1; Jake falls to 0-4.**

Bijan Robinson and Kyren Williams each scored **26.70**. Their identical returns gave Devin **53.40** at the top of his running-back group, and Bijan's [145 rushing yards and two touchdowns on Monday night](https://www.nfl.com/news/falcons-saints-on-monday-night-football-what-we-learned-from-atlanta-s-45-24-win) helped Devin finish just ahead of Rob for the weekly high score.

The supporting points came from across the lineup: Lamar Jackson **18.88**, Dak Prescott **18.10**, Will Reichard **17.00**, Tee Higgins **15.70**, and George Kittle **13.00**. Devin reached 209.48 with Jaxon Smith-Njigba and Mike Evans each at **7.60**, Amon-Ra St. Brown at **7.50**, and Jadarian Price at zero. The previous week's receiver-led explosion became a running-back-led win.

Kyle Monangai scored **27.00** on Devin's bench. Replacing Price with Monangai would have added those 27 points directly; Devin won by 74.80 without them. That is an unusually comfortable way to leave your roster's best individual score sitting idle.

Jake's Bryce Young gave him **21.46**, Puka Nacua **18.70**, and Malik Nabers **17.20**. Those three accounted for **57.36** of his total. The bottom of the lineup offered almost nothing: Saquon Barkley **1.00**, Kaelon Black **1.30**, Michael Pittman **1.50**, Josh Downs **1.60**, and Kalif Raymond **0.60**, with Ladd McConkey and Kenyon Sadiq both at zero. Barkley left the Rams game early; the injury compounded a lineup already running short on production.

The current nameplate reads **Crazy J's Bed & Breakfast**, replacing Stable of Stars. The exact rename date is unavailable, but the Week 4 result fits the new sign: Devin checked in, collected the largest win of the week, and encountered very little resistance. Jake's two championships remain in the record book. This season's first win remains missing.

### Brian 205.70, Travon 153.92: The Receiver Room Reopens

**Tri-Lams rebounds to 3-1; Von drops to 1-3.**

Brian climbed **63.48 points** above his Week 3 total. CeeDee Lamb's **24.30** and Nico Collins' **23.80** drove the recovery, while Emanuel Wilson supplied **24.00**. Those three contributed **72.10**.

Lamb and Collins were central to the same [Cowboys-Texans game](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-season-week-4-what-we-learned-from-sunday-s-games): Lamb had 189 receiving yards and a touchdown, and Collins returned with 117 yards and two touchdowns. Brian had a productive interest in both sides of that shootout.

Ollie Gordon added **16.00** in his first weekly start for Brian after a September 30 waiver claim. Ka'imi Fairbairn also scored **16.00**, Seattle's defense supplied **12.00**, and Christian McCaffrey and Deebo Samuel each contributed **13.00**. Dalton Kincaid's **0.70** and DJ Moore's **1.70** were frustrating small returns inside an otherwise substantial win.

Travon had the quarterback advantage I expected: Brock Purdy and Patrick Mahomes combined for **36.62**, compared with **25.80** from Brian's Jordan Love and Michael Penix. It was worth **10.82**, nowhere near enough to offset Brian's work elsewhere. Jonathan Taylor led Travon at **21.70**, Quinshon Judkins scored **15.60**, and Chris Olave added **11.60**. Trey McBride's **3.10**, David Montgomery's **4.30**, Quentin Johnston's **4.00**, and Tyjae Spears' **0.60** left too much ground to recover.

Brian keeps his place in the 3-1 group after a one-week interruption. Travon has lost two straight since beating Jake in Week 2. I picked the quarterbacks to carry Von; Brian's receivers and running backs carried the result.

### Eric 198.40, Dave 145.90: McMillan Ends the Perfect Start

**Boutte on Sunday reaches 2-2 with a second straight win; Early and out of hope drops to 3-1.**

Tetairoa McMillan jumped from **1.70** in Week 3 to **31.20** in Week 4. Eric's quarterbacks made the swing stick: Joe Burrow scored **23.72** and C.J. Stroud **23.08**, combining for **46.80**. McMillan and the quarterbacks supplied **78.00** before the rest of the lineup was counted.

Spencer Shrader added **14.00** after Eric acquired him on September 30. Jahmyr Gibbs delivered **13.70**, Tucker Kraft **10.50**, and Jameson Williams **10.20**. Last week Gibbs led the league; this week McMillan did the heavy lifting. Eric's consecutive wins have come with different players carrying the biggest load.

Dave's Josh Allen returned **18.52**, Alvin Kamara **17.80**, Sam LaPorta **14.40**, and the Giants defense **14.00**. Matthew Stafford's **9.68** left Dave well behind Eric at quarterback. Rashee Rice scored zero, Emeka Egbuka **1.30**, and Travis Kelce **1.50**. Allen and Kamara could only cover so many quiet slots.

Kirk Cousins' **22.60**, Romeo Doubs' **17.80**, and T.J. Hockenson's **11.90** sat on Dave's bench. Kirk over Stafford, Doubs over Rice, and Hockenson over Kelce would have added **41.12**. Those three obvious swaps still leave an **11.38-point** deficit against Eric's actual total. Dave had room to improve his lineup; Eric also earned a genuinely large win.

I expected Dave to withstand Eric's Gibbs-led counterpunch. Gibbs wasn't even Eric's largest threat. McMillan and the quarterback pair turned the upset into a rout.

## Around the League

### The Full Javonte Deal

The September 30 trade sent **Javonte Williams and Jake's own 2027 ninth-round pick to Rob**. Jake received **Kenny Gainwell and Rob's own 2027 third-round pick**. No FAAB was included.

Williams started immediately for Rob and scored 26.30. Gainwell scored **8.70** on Jake's bench. That is the first week's return, with the draft picks still ahead of both owners. Rob obtained an immediate starter after losing De'Von Achane for the season; Jake moved value into a future third-round selection and a different running back. Week 4 made Rob's side easy to see.

The waiver board produced useful starters too. Brian's Ollie Gordon scored **16.00**, Eric's Spencer Shrader **14.00**, and Jake's new Pittsburgh defense **8.00**. Rob claimed Drake Maye on October 2 after Michael dropped him; Maye's **26.16** remained on Rob's bench. Michael's newly acquired Marcus Mariota also stayed on the bench, scoring **5.34** before his game exit.

### The Injury Bill

The [official Week 4 status report](https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-week-4-injury-report-player-statuses-for-all-16-games) confirms the pregame absences that affected these lineups: Michael's Jefferson (ankle) and Smith (hamstring), and Devin's Price (chest), all scored zero as starters. Michael kept Breece Hall (quadriceps) on the bench, Jake kept Baker Mayfield (right thumb) there, and Rob benched Caleb Williams (hamstring); all three were ruled out for Week 4. Terry McLaurin, on Travon's bench, was also inactive after a hamstring concern, according to the [Sunday takeaways](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-season-week-4-what-we-learned-from-sunday-s-games).

The games added several consequential exits:

- **Devin's Lamar Jackson** injured his ankle late in the first half against Tennessee and did not return. The [dated NFL report](https://www.nfl.com/news/ravens-lamar-jackson-ankle-injury-titans) quotes his coach awaiting further assessment and reports Ian Rapoport's day-to-day expectation.
- **Rob's Ja'Marr Chase** left Cincinnati's game with a concussion in the first half, contributing only 2.70. The [NFL game takeaways](https://www.nfl.com/news/2026-nfl-season-week-4-what-we-learned-from-sunday-s-games) confirm the concussion.
- **Jake's Saquon Barkley** was out for more than three quarters against the Rams. The same NFL report confirms the early exit; it does not specify a body part in that account.
- **Michael's Marcus Mariota** left the London game after a first-quarter sack. The [NFL report](https://www.nfl.com/news/commanders-qb-marcus-mariota-knee-injury-colts-athan-kaliakmanis) describes the right-leg contact, and the Sunday takeaways identify an aggravated MCL injury.

Monday's injury context mattered even to players who stayed on the field. Atlanta lost left tackle Jake Matthews early, while New Orleans entered without Kaden Elliss, Carl Granderson, and Anfernee Jennings. The [Monday recap](https://www.nfl.com/news/falcons-saints-on-monday-night-football-what-we-learned-from-atlanta-s-45-24-win) documents those absences alongside the Robinsons' five rushing touchdowns.
