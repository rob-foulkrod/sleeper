## Intro

Michael finally turned steady scoring into a win. Broken leg broken team beat Von by **53.80 points**, ended a four-game losing streak and climbed to sixth at **1-4**, pushing Travon to seventh with the same record. Michael has cleared 150 points in all five weeks; this time, the production bought him breathing room. His next opponent is Jake, whose newly renamed Crazy J’s Bed & Breakfast remains winless. The bottom of The League has begun to separate, and Week 6 gives Michael a chance to make that movement stick.

Depth becomes the story as bye weeks pull established scorers out of lineups. Rob supplied the loudest example of its value, posting **287.10 points** in the week’s highest-scoring matchup and extending Unstoppable Farce’s perfect start to **5-0**. Now the scoring leader faces Ja’Marr Chase’s bye, while other contenders confront longer replacement lists. The next useful waiver addition could matter considerably more than another impressive name with Sunday off.

## Game by Game

### Broken leg broken team (192.64) vs Von  (138.84)

**Michael Gets His First Win by 53.80 Points.**

Michael defeated Travon **192.64–138.84**, claiming his first win of the season with a **53.80-point** blowout. After four losses, Broken leg broken team finally had enough production across the lineup to turn a strong score into a result. Michael finished **#4** in weekly scoring; Travon finished **#7**.

Jared Goff led Michael with **28.94 points**, throwing for 271 yards and three touchdowns while adding a rushing score. Parker Washington supplied **24.40** on six catches for 124 yards and two touchdowns, comfortably exceeding his 8.78-point projection. The backfield reinforced that work: James Cook scored **21.80** with 111 rushing yards and two touchdowns, and Braelon Allen delivered **17.60** with 116 rushing yards and a touchdown. Tyler Warren’s **14.40** gave Michael another useful contribution despite several quiet receiving slots.

Travon got **24.28** from Jacoby Brissett and **19.00** from Jason Myers, but Jonathan Taylor’s **8.20** and David Montgomery’s **1.10** left the running game short of Michael’s output. Roman Wilson and Quentin Johnston both scored zero, adding holes that Brissett’s three passing touchdowns could only cover so far. Travon’s losing streak reached three games. **Michael moved to 1-4 (Rank #6), while Travon fell to 1-4 (Rank #7).**

### Tri-Lams (201.88) vs Crazy J’s Bed & Breakfast (126.52)

**Brian Wins by 75.36 as Bucky Irving Powers Tri-Lams.**

Brian defeated Jake **201.88 to 126.52**, delivering a **75.36-point** blowout and the league’s **#2** score for the week. Bucky Irving drove the result with **29.50 points**, rushing for **165 yards and a touchdown** on 21 carries and adding a receiving touchdown. That gave Brian a substantial foundation on a week when several of his receivers contributed very little.

Jordan Love supplied **25.28 points** behind 367 passing yards and two touchdowns, while Isaiah Likely turned 11 catches, 94 yards and two touchdowns into **21.40**. Ka’imi Fairbairn and Houston’s defense each added **16.00**. Those contributions carried Tri-Lams comfortably through CeeDee Lamb’s **0.90**, Deebo Samuel’s **0.50** and Jaylen Waddle’s **2.80**. Brian followed last week’s receiver-led win with production from a different set of starters.

Jake got **20.70 points** from Puka Nacua, whose 17 catches on 24 targets produced 138 receiving yards and a touchdown. Malik Nabers added **18.10**, but the quarterback slots yielded **8.30** from Sam Darnold and **7.92** from Bo Nix, and Pittsburgh’s defense managed **1.00**. Jake finished **#8** in weekly scoring, extending a difficult opening stretch. **Brian moved to 4-1 (Rank #3), while Jake fell to 0-5 (Rank #8).**

### Unstoppable Farce (287.10) vs Boutte on Sunday (195.74)

**Rob Posts the Weekly High and Wins by 91.36.**

Rob’s Unstoppable Farce defeated Eric’s Boutte on Sunday **287.10 to 195.74**, delivering the highest score in the eight-team league and a **91.36-point** win. Drake Maye led the matchup with **39.46 points**: 374 passing yards, four passing touchdowns and a rushing touchdown. That gave Rob a towering lead contribution, with substantial production spread across the lineup.

Jaylen Warren supplied **30.80**, rushing for 101 yards and a touchdown while catching six passes for 87 yards and another score. Trevor Lawrence added **21.74**, Derrick Henry **20.00**, and George Pickens **19.00** on nine catches for 130 yards and a touchdown. Rob had enough scoring depth to turn an excellent quarterback performance into a blowout.

Eric finished **#3 in weekly scoring** and still lost by 91.36—a particularly expensive week to draw Rob. Jahmyr Gibbs earned his **32.70 points** with 151 rushing yards, two rushing touchdowns and 56 receiving yards. Joe Burrow contributed **24.60**, and Spencer Shrader supplied **20.00**, but Eric’s strong total was overwhelmed by the week’s top offense. **Rob moved to 5-0 (Rank #1), while Eric fell to 2-3 (Rank #5).**

### Amon Another level  (187.84) vs Early and out of hope (170.10)

**Devin’s supporting points carry a 17.74-point win over Dave.**

Devin defeated Dave **187.84 to 170.10**, getting enough production across his lineup to overcome Dave’s bigger individual performances. Aaron Rodgers led Devin with **22.00 points**, throwing for 235 yards and three touchdowns without an interception. Mike Evans supplied **19.30** and Jaxon Smith-Njigba **17.80**; both topped 100 receiving yards and scored a touchdown.

Dave had the matchup’s leading scorer in Josh Allen, whose **30.50 points** included 121 rushing yards and a rushing touchdown. Matthew Stafford added **27.32**, and Christian Watson delivered **26.30** behind 143 receiving yards and two touchdowns. The thinner returns elsewhere proved costly: Alvin Kamara scored **0.60**, T.J. Hockenson **0.70**, and Romeo Doubs **1.60**. Devin found useful separation in Will Reichard’s **15.00** and the Jets defense’s **17.00**, with Aaron Jones adding **15.70**.

These were the week’s **#5 and #6 scores** in the eight-team league, enough for Devin to extend his winning streak to three while Dave took his second consecutive loss. **Devin moved to 4-1 (Rank #2), while Dave fell to 3-2 (Rank #4).**
